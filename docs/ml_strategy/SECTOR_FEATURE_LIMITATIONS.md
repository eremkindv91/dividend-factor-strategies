# Sector Feature Limitations

- No audited Brent/Urals, steel/input-price or mortgage-vintage history is configured.
- USD/RUB is an FX interaction, not a replacement for oil or steel prices.
- Key rate and RGBI are rate-cycle interactions, not issuer-level bank or developer fundamentals.
- Issuer exposure weights are blocked until a versioned disclosure supplies `published_at` and `available_at`.
- The historical security master is still not a complete listing/delisting database, so residual survivorship risk remains.
- Pack ablation is research evidence, not causal attribution.
- No pack passed promotion on the current fixed test. The UI therefore shows no stock-level production sector drivers.
